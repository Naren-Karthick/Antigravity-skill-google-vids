# Timeline Editing Reference

## Timeline preparation
- Confirm the scene order against the storyboard.
- Confirm the target aspect ratio and total runtime.
- Import/insert the approved narration and scene assets.
- Keep a scene-to-asset mapping in the manifest.

## Scene alignment
1. Use actual narration duration where audio exists.
2. Start the visual beat at the intended narration phrase.
3. Leave enough visual tail for the viewer to understand the action.
4. Avoid cutting words, breaths, or important sound effects at boundaries.
5. Trim dead time; do not shorten a shot so much that the action becomes unreadable.
6. Use speed changes sparingly and inspect motion quality after adjustment.

## Audio tracks
- Narration should remain intelligible and dominant.
- Music should support the mood without masking speech.
- SFX should reinforce visible actions and be timed deliberately.
- Mute source clip audio only when it conflicts with the intended mix.
- Check starts, ends, fades, and overlaps.

## Captions and titles
- Verify captions against the final narration.
- Correct names, technical terms, punctuation, and numbers.
- Keep text within platform-safe margins.
- Check readability on a small/mobile preview.
- Avoid placing text over faces or essential visual information.

## Transitions and motion
Use consistent transitions and restrained object animation. Prefer direct cuts when they serve the pacing. Avoid adding motion to every object merely because the control exists.

## Final timeline review
- Inspect scene order and duration.
- Inspect every track for gaps, accidental overlaps, or orphaned media.
- Preview the complete timeline from beginning to end.
- Re-check synchronization after any later scene move or trim.
