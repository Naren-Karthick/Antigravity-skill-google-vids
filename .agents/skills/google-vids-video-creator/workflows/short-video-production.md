# Short Video Production Workflow

Use for Shorts, Reels, TikTok, and other concise vertical videos.

## Defaults
- Prefer vertical 9:16 unless the user specifies otherwise.
- Make the first seconds immediately understandable and curiosity-driven.
- Keep one central idea and a focused narrative.
- Use large, concise on-screen text and captions that remain readable on mobile.
- Runtime is user-defined; do not assume a platform maximum.

## Workflow
1. Define one viewer takeaway and one target audience.
2. Research factual claims and choose a single hook.
3. Write a concise script with a clear payoff; read it aloud and measure narration.
4. Create a fast storyboard with visually distinct beats.
5. Establish a character/style reference if using recurring animation or stickmen.
6. Generate a pilot shot and check visual consistency.
7. Generate remaining shots in small batches and track them in the manifest.
8. Add narration, captions, music, and only necessary SFX.
9. Preview at phone-sized scale; check safe margins and caption readability.
10. Export and validate the MP4.

## Pacing
Change visual information when the narration or story beat changes. Use cuts, camera changes, movement, and occasional emphasis rather than constant random motion. Do not make every shot excessively short.

## Final checks
Hook is clear without context; captions are readable; the ending delivers the promised payoff; no important word is clipped; the export has the requested orientation and runtime.
