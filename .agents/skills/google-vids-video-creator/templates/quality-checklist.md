# Video Quality Checklist

## Brief and story
- [ ] Audience, language, platform, and requested format are correct.
- [ ] Opening hook is clear and engaging.
- [ ] Narrative is coherent and matches the brief.
- [ ] Factual claims are checked; speculation is clearly framed.
- [ ] Ending feels intentional and includes CTA only when appropriate.

## Visuals
- [ ] Correct aspect ratio and orientation.
- [ ] Character/style continuity maintained.
- [ ] All scenes present and in intended order.
- [ ] No blank frames, placeholders, accidental duplicates, or unwanted artifacts.
- [ ] Framing and text are safe for target platform/mobile viewing.
- [ ] Motion and transitions are smooth and purposeful.

## Audio
- [ ] Narration is complete and intelligible.
- [ ] Pronunciation of names, terms, and numbers is correct.
- [ ] No clipped words, awkward gaps, or unintended overlaps.
- [ ] Music and SFX do not overpower narration.
- [ ] No accidental conflicting clip audio or clipping.

## Captions and text
- [ ] Captions match actual spoken words.
- [ ] Spelling, punctuation, names, and numbers are correct.
- [ ] Text is readable and does not cover faces/key content.

## Export
- [ ] Export completed and file exists at exact path.
- [ ] File size is non-zero.
- [ ] Metadata matches target dimensions and expected duration.
- [ ] Playback checked at opening, middle, transitions, and ending; full playback when practical.
- [ ] Editable project/source assets retained when possible.

Record each item as pass, fail, or not verified. Never silently treat not verified as pass.
